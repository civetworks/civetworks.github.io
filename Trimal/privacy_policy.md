# Privacy Policy for Trimal

**Last updated:** December 08, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in Trimal.

## Collection and Use of Information

**We do not collect, use, save, or have access to any of your personal data recorded in Trimal.**

The individual settings and time zone data you configure in the Trimal app are stored only on your device. We have no access to this data, and it is not shared with anyone.

## Data Retention

Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not retain any data on our servers. All your data is retained locally on your device and can be deleted by you at any time by deleting the application or removing the specific entries within the app.

## Third-Party Services

Trimal does not use any third-party services for analytics, advertising, or data collection.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at civetworks@outlook.com.
