# Privacy Policy for Trimal

**Last updated:** September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in Trimal.

## Collection and Use of Information

**Trimal does not operate an account system, analytics service, or advertising service. We do not receive your saved cities, currency preferences, or entered amounts on our own servers.**

Your saved cities, app preferences, selected currencies, recent currency choices, and last valid conversion amount are stored on your device. Downloaded reference rates are also stored locally so they can be used offline. Currency calculations take place on your device; entered amounts are never included in exchange-rate requests.

## Data Retention

Trimal does not retain your app data on our servers. You can remove saved cities within the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored settings and exchange-rate cache.

## Third-Party Services

Trimal uses external services to provide specific features:

- Apple's MapKit receives searches for cities and places. WeatherKit receives the coordinates of saved places when requesting weather.
- Frankfurter receives requests for its currency catalog and reference exchange rates. Rate requests include the selected base currency code, but not the amount you enter, your saved city list, or an account identifier. Network requests also expose information needed to deliver the response, such as a network address, to the service infrastructure.

These services process requests under their own policies. See [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the [Frankfurter API privacy information](https://frankfurter.dev/#faq). Trimal does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
