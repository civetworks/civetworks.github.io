# MetroMinute Support

Fast rail departures for nearby or selected stations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which regions and services does MetroMinute support?

MetroMinute supports MTA Subway and PATH in New York City, BART and Caltrain in the San Francisco Bay Area, MBTA rapid transit in Boston, and CTA 'L' service in Chicago.

### Do I have to share my location?

No. Location access is optional. You can select New York City, the San Francisco Bay Area, Boston, or Chicago manually, then search for any supported station in that area.

### How does nearest-station mode work?

When you allow location access, MetroMinute uses a current foreground location fix to select a supported metro area and find the nearest supported rail station. MetroMinute does not use background location or build a location history.

### How do I choose a different station?

Tap the search button in the top-right corner. Search by station, line, or rail system, then select a result. MetroMinute also keeps up to four recent stations per supported area on your device.

### Why are there multiple system tabs at some stations?

Some reviewed station complexes connect more than one supported rail system. MetroMinute shows each system in a separate tab so its arrivals, routes, and service status stay clear.

### Why are arrivals missing or delayed?

MetroMinute displays live information supplied by transit providers. A provider may delay, change, or temporarily stop publishing data. Pull down to refresh, check your connection, and follow official station signage and agency guidance.

### Why are Chicago or Caltrain arrivals unavailable?

Those services depend on provider-issued API access configured in the published build. Search and bundled station information can remain available even if a live feed is temporarily unavailable.

### Does MetroMinute plan trips or show a map?

No. MetroMinute is intentionally focused on upcoming departures at one nearby or selected station. It does not provide maps or trip planning.
