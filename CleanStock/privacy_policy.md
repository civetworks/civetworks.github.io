# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** May 17, 2026

Clean Stock ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Clean Stock mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect

Clean Stock does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, payment information, contacts, photos, location, or health data.

The watchlists, list names, and ticker symbols you create are stored locally on your device using Apple's app storage. We do not receive your watchlists unless you choose to send information to us when requesting support.

## Market, Fund, and News Data

When you search for tickers, refresh prices, view charts, open stock or ETF details, load holdings, compare benchmarks, or open news, the App sends the relevant ticker symbols and request details to market, fund, and news data providers so the requested information can be returned.

Those providers may receive technical information normally included with network requests, such as your IP address, device type, operating system, app version, and network metadata. Their handling of that information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

## AI and Sentiment Features

The public App Store build of Clean Stock does not expose AI sentiment features.

Local developer builds may include a sentiment feature for personal testing. That local-only feature can send selected news headlines and related ticker context to a configured AI provider for analysis. This feature is not available in the public App Store release.

## Analytics and Advertising

Clean Stock does not include third-party advertising.

Clean Stock does not use third-party analytics to track you across apps or websites.

## How We Use Information

Information handled by the App is used to:

- Save and display your local watchlists.
- Search for market symbols.
- Retrieve quotes, charts, benchmark comparisons, ETF holdings, fund details, and news.
- Respond to support requests if you contact us.

## Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share your local watchlists with third parties except as needed to request the market, fund, or news data you ask the App to load.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users, or the security of the App.

## Data Storage and Retention

Watchlists and tracked tickers are stored locally on your device. You can remove tickers in the App, delete lists in the App where supported, or delete the App to remove locally stored app data from your device.

We do not operate a Clean Stock account system or central watchlist database.

## Children's Privacy

Clean Stock is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Financial Disclaimer

Clean Stock provides market information for general informational purposes only. It does not provide investment, financial, tax, legal, or other professional advice. Market data may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
