# Support for Civet Works ChatGPT Plugins

For help or feedback, email [civetworks@outlook.com](mailto:civetworks@outlook.com). Support is provided by Hongfei Zhou under the Civet Works name. Chinese and English messages are welcome.

This page supports **旅游规划师 (Travel Planner)**, **景点故事导游 (Landmark Guide)**, and **美食爱好者 (Food Finder)**. Include the plugin name, the OpenAI product you are using, and a brief description of the issue. A short example or a screenshot with private details removed is usually enough. Do not send passwords, API keys, identification documents, or a complete private conversation.

## Getting Started

Use the relevant plugin in a compatible OpenAI product:

- **旅游规划师:** Provide a city and your interests for travel ideas, attractions, neighborhoods, museums, exhibitions, and places to eat. Example: 帮我规划东京 5 天旅行.
- **景点故事导游:** Provide a landmark and a topic, such as architecture, art, history, or a local story. Example: 介绍一下故宫的历史故事. It responds in Chinese and aims for a conversational explanation of roughly three minutes when spoken.
- **美食爱好者:** Provide a city, area, venue name, or food preference to find and compare restaurants, cafes, and bars. Example: 推荐东京涩谷值得去的咖啡厅.

You can refine the question, ask for sources, or request a shorter explanation. The directory determines which plugins are currently available to your account.

## Search, Audio, and Availability

Search and voice capabilities depend on the host product and the tools available in your conversation. These plugins do not supply separate search or audio services. If audio is unavailable, you can still use a landmark guide's text as a narration script.

Installation, account access, product availability, and OpenAI billing are managed by OpenAI. For those issues, use the help options in the OpenAI product you are using.

## Accuracy and Scope

Generated explanations can contain mistakes or mix a traditional story with established history. Recommendations, ratings, prices, opening hours, and reservation requirements can be outdated. Ask for sources and confirm important details with an authoritative source or the venue.

The plugins do not provide bookings or ticket purchases. Use Travel Planner for travel planning; Landmark Guide focuses on the stories behind a place.

## Privacy Requests

Use the support email above to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent to Civet Works. The developer cannot view or delete your OpenAI conversation history through these plugins. See the [Privacy Policy](/ChatGPT/privacy_policy/) for details.
