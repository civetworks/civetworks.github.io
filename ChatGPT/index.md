# ChatGPT Plugins by Civet Works

Travel ideas, landmark stories, and places to eat. These plugins are provided by **Hongfei Zhou**, under the **Civet Works** name, for use in compatible OpenAI products.

## 旅游规划师 · Travel Planner

Explore a city through its sights, neighborhoods, shops, museums, exhibitions, restaurants, cafes, and bars. Provide your destination and interests to get travel ideas, reasons to visit, reservation information, and practical notes.

Example: **帮我规划东京 5 天旅行**

## 景点故事导游 · Landmark Guide

Understand the history, culture, architecture, art, and people behind a landmark through Chinese storytelling. Ask about a place or a particular story for a short, conversational guide that is easy to read or use as a narration script. This plugin focuses on landmark stories rather than travel routes.

Example: **介绍一下故宫的历史故事**

## 美食爱好者 · Food Finder

Find and compare restaurants, cafes, and bars by city, area, venue name, or food preference. Get addresses, price and rating information, reasons to visit, and dishes to consider.

Example: **推荐东京涩谷值得去的咖啡厅**

## Using the Plugins

Installation and public availability are managed through OpenAI's plugin directory. Search and audio features depend on the tools available in the OpenAI product you are using; these plugins do not provide separate search or audio services.

Generated information can be inaccurate or outdated. Confirm important details, prices, opening hours, and reservation requirements with the venue or an authoritative source. The plugins do not sell tickets or make bookings.

These are independently developed plugins, not OpenAI products or official services of the places they describe.
