# Privacy Policy for Civet Works ChatGPT Plugins

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026

This policy covers the following plugins provided by **Hongfei Zhou**, under the **Civet Works** name, and their documentation and support pages in this directory:

- **旅游规划师 · Travel Planner** — city travel ideas and planning.
- **景点故事导游 · Landmark Guide** — landmark history and cultural storytelling.
- **美食爱好者 · Food Finder** — restaurant, cafe, and bar discovery.

## Information Used by the Plugins

The current versions of these plugins contain instructions, skills, and listing assets. They do not connect to a developer-operated server or MCP service, create separate accounts, or include advertising, tracking, analytics, or automatic reporting to the developer.

You provide a city or area, landmark or venue name, interests, preferences, and any questions or context you choose to include in the OpenAI product where you use a plugin. The host uses that information to generate a travel plan, explanation, or recommendation. You do not need to provide identification documents, booking details, precise location coordinates, or account credentials.

The developer does not receive or store your chat messages, uploaded files, voice recordings, or generated answers through these plugins. There is no developer-hosted copy of your conversation or a separate plugin account to delete.

## OpenAI and Other Services

ChatGPT or Codex processes the content you provide and supplies the model and available tools. If you use search, voice, or another connected service, that feature may process the information needed to carry out your request under its own permissions and policies. These services are not operated by Civet Works.

OpenAI's processing, retention, and use of data are governed by its applicable terms, [Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), and your product or workspace settings. This policy does not promise zero retention or no use for model improvement by OpenAI or another service provider. Manage conversation history, privacy settings, and provider-held data through the relevant service.

If you open an external link, the destination website handles your visit under its own policies.

## Support Correspondence

If you email [civetworks@outlook.com](mailto:civetworks@outlook.com), the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used only to respond to your request and handle necessary follow-up. You do not need to send a complete chat transcript to ask for help.

Support correspondence is handled by the developer and the Outlook email service operated by Microsoft. Avoid sending passwords, API keys, identification documents, or unrelated personal information. We do not sell or rent support information or use it for advertising or profiling.

We retain support correspondence while handling your request and necessary follow-up. You can use the same email address to request access, correction, or deletion. Information may need to be retained where required by law; deletion of our correspondence does not delete copies independently held by a service provider under its own policies.

## This Website

GitHub Pages hosts these public documents. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking pixels to these pages. GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. See [GitHub Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection) and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for its processing and retention practices.

Reading a documentation page does not send your ChatGPT or Codex conversation to this website. Hosting providers, OpenAI, and the support email provider may process information in different countries under their respective policies.

## Your Choices

- Choose what information to include in a request and use the privacy and deletion controls offered by your OpenAI product or workspace.
- Disable or uninstall a plugin through the host if you no longer want to use its instructions. This does not delete existing conversations or records held by other providers.
- Contact us about support information held by Civet Works. Requests about OpenAI or GitHub data should be directed to those providers through their policies linked above.

Use of these plugins is subject to the host service's age and account eligibility requirements. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can address the request.

## Security and Changes

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. This policy will be updated if a covered plugin's data handling changes, including if a future version adds a developer-operated service. The revised date will appear at the top of this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email [civetworks@outlook.com](mailto:civetworks@outlook.com). Chinese and English messages are welcome.
