### Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I restore my purchase?**  
Go to Settings > Credits and tap the "Restore Purchases" button at the bottom of the screen.

**Where are my diaries stored?**  
Your diaries are stored locally on your device to ensure your privacy. If you delete the app, your local diaries will be deleted.

**Why did the AI generation fail?**  
AI generation requires an active internet connection. Please check your network and try again. If the problem persists, please contact support.
