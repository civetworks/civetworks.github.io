### Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I restore my purchase?**  
Go to Settings > Credits and tap the "Restore Purchases" button at the bottom of the screen.

**Where are my diaries stored?**  
Your diaries are stored locally on your device to ensure your privacy. If you delete the app, your local diaries will be deleted.

**Is my data private?**  
We do not sell your personal information. Your diaries and generated content are primarily stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud container. We do not store your personal diaries on our central servers.

**How is my data used for AI generation?**  
When you use AI features, strictly necessary data (such as selected photos and text prompts) is sent to third-party AI providers (e.g., Google Gemini, OpenAI) via secure APIs solely to generate your content. We direct these providers not to use your data for training their general public models.

**Does Mementi access all my photos?**  
No. We access your photo library only when you select images to create diaries or posts. We do not upload your entire library to our servers.

**Why did the AI generation fail?**  
AI generation requires an active internet connection. Please check your network and try again. If the problem persists, please contact support.
